# Third-party data and tools

CausalFrontier's documentation discusses public biomedical databases, literature,
research software, and external identifiers. This repository does not bundle their
records, articles, code, or database snapshots. References and identifiers do not
grant a license to retrieve, redistribute, or reuse provider content.

The public example is an original, synthetic, network-free aggregate fixture. Anyone
publishing an external evidence receipt or capsule must review the exact fields,
retain source provenance, comply with current provider terms, and keep restricted or
patient-level material out of the public repository.

Hashes and identifiers establish byte identity or documentary linkage only. They do
not establish biological truth, rights, source independence, temporal validity, or
permission to perform an experiment.
