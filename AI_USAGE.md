# AI-use disclosure

Generative AI has assisted this project's software development, test generation,
documentation, adversarial review, and research planning. Known systems include
OpenAI Codex-based development sessions. ToolUniverse has also been used as a
tool-discovery and public biomedical retrieval layer; its returned records are
inputs to human review, not autonomous scientific authority.

Historical development did not preserve a complete, repository-bound record of
every model identifier, runtime version, prompt, or accepted edit. That missing
provenance is a publication-readiness limitation. Future journal submissions
must identify the tools and versions used for code and manuscript work, describe
the scope of assistance, and be reviewed, edited, and validated by accountable
human authors.

AI systems are not authors. They cannot accept responsibility for the work,
approve a scientific claim, determine novelty, declare a benchmark independent,
or make an editorial recommendation. All such judgments remain human decisions.
